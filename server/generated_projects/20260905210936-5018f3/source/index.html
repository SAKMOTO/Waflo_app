About Us

Local Riders. Real Trails.

Founded in 2014 by two friends who lived at the base of Cedar Ridge, TrailBike Rentals started with three bikes and a hand-painted map. Today, our shop is still family-run, still independent, and still obsessed with sending riders home tired, smiling, and a little dustier than they arrived.

Every bike in our fleet is tuned by certified mechanics before each rental. We fit you properly, share the trail conditions for the day, and point you toward the lines we love most.

  • Safety First

    Helmets, lights, and a pre-ride safety briefing are included with every rental.

  • Certified Guides

    Book a guided ride and let a local expert lead you to the best lines and viewpoints.

  • Trail Knowledge

    Daily condition reports and maps drawn by riders, for riders.
