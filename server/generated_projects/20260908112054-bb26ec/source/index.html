Sourcing Philosophy

From High-Altitude Farms Direct to Your Cup

At Ember & Bean, we believe exceptional coffee starts with ethical relationships. We partner directly with smallholder farmers across East Africa, Central, and South America, paying premiums well above fair-trade minimums.

Every harvest is meticulously cupped and profiled. We roast on our custom cast-iron drum roaster, carefully controlling heat vectors to bring out bright florals, rich cocoa undertones, and clean, lingering finishes.
