The Roastery Philosophy

At Lumen Roast, coffee isn't just a caffeine delivery mechanism—it's a sensory journey that connects distant soil, dedicated hands, and mindful moments. Founded by roastmaster David Vance in 2012, our roastery was established on three core pillars:

  • Direct Farm Relationships

    We work directly with single-estate growers, paying 35% above Fair Trade minimums to ensure sustainable farming livelihoods.

  • Precision Thermal Profiling

    Every harvest batch is test-roasted to pinpoint its unique thermal sweet spot, highlighting sweet floral or rich cacao profiles.

  • Zero Waste Packaging

    All our coffee bags and mailing materials are 100% home-compostable, keeping our footprint light on the earth.
