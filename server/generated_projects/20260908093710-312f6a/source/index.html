Our Heritage

From High-Altitude Farms to Your Morning Cup

Founded in 2016, Bean & Brew began with a simple vision: honoring the meticulous journey of the coffee bean. We form direct trade relationships with eco-friendly micro-farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

Every morning, our master roasters meticulously sample and heat batches in our vintage cast-iron roaster to pull out notes of wild berries, velvet dark chocolate, and roasted hazelnut.

Direct Trade Ethically Sourced

We pay 35% above fair-trade prices directly to farmer collectives.

Zero-Waste Bakehouse

Our pastries are handcrafted using locally farmed butter and zero preservatives.
