Sanctuary Guidelines

Cat Etiquette & House Rules

To ensure our resident and adoptable cats feel safe, happy, and respected, please review our lounge guidelines before your visit.

Cats need plenty of sleep! Please do not wake a sleeping cat, pull their tail, or pick them up forcibly. If a cat approaches your lap or seat on their own terms, enjoy the purrs!

Felines have sensitive eyes. Photos and videos are strongly encouraged, but please ensure your camera flash is turned off completely at all times.

Human treats like chocolate, coffee, or dairy can be toxic to cats. You may only feed cats designated snacks purchased directly from our menu under staff guidance.

Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult (1:2 ratio). All guests must sanitize hands before entering and after leaving the feline lounge area.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

If you connect with an adoptable cat during your visit, fill out an inquiry form with our lounge staff or online. Our shelter partner reviews applications within 24–48 hours to ensure a perfect match!

We welcome walk-ins whenever space permits! However, because lounge capacity is strictly limited to 14 guests at a time for feline comfort, online reservations are strongly recommended on weekends.

For territory management and health safety, personal pets are strictly not allowed inside the cafe or lounge space. All resident felines are fully vaccinated and health-screened.
